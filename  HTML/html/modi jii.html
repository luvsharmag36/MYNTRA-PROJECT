EARLY LIFE AND RISE TO POWER

Narendra Modi was born on September 17, 1950, in Vadnagar, Gujarat, into a modest family. In his youth, he helped his father sell tea at a railway station before joining the nationalist organization Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh (RSS) full-time. He later moved into politics with the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP). Modi rose through party ranks and served as the Chief Minister of Gujarat from 2001 to 2014, where he gained a reputation for focusing on economic development and industrial growth.

Mission Year Achievement

ISRO Mars Orbiter Mission (Mangalyaan) 2014 India became the first country to reach Mars orbit on its first attempt. The spacecraft entered Mars orbit in September 2014.

Indigenous Cryogenic Engine (GSLV-D5) 2014 First successful flight using an indigenous cryogenic engine, a major milestone for India's launch capability.

Navigation with Indian Constellation (NavIC) completion 2018 India completed its own regional satellite navigation system, reducing dependence on GPS for regional services.

PSLV-C37 2017 Successfully launched 104 satellites in a single mission, setting a world record at the time.

Chandrayaan-2 2019 Orbiter was successful and continues scientific observations, although the Vikram lander crashed during landing.

SSLV-D2 2023 First successful operational demonstration of ISRO's Small Satellite Launch Vehicle after the initial test failure.

Chandrayaan-3 2023 India became the first country to land near the Moon's south pole and the fourth country to achieve a soft landing on the Moon.

Aditya-L1 2023–24 India's first mission dedicated to studying the Sun from the L1 Lagrange point.

XPoSat 2024 India's first dedicated X-ray polarimetry mission to study black holes, neutron stars, and other cosmic X-ray sources.

SpaDeX (Space Docking Experiment) 2024–25 India successfully demonstrated satellite docking technology, becoming the fourth country to achieve this capability.

Chief Minister and Prime Minister

Modi served as the Chief Minister of Gujarat from 2001 to 2014, focusing on business growth and development. In 2014, he led the BJP to win a massive victory in the general elections and became India's Prime Minister. He won re-election in 2019 and again in 2024, focusing on digital growth, infrastructure, and national security.
